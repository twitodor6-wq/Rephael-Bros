- Only the first bank falling-tree encounter and elevated far-bank rotten shelf have collapse behavior. Trees in the painted background remain decorative.
- Exit-root arch geometry is procedural and still visually simpler than approved lush swamp imagery; final sprite design is pending.
- Physical movement of webs is intentionally simulated ONLY by a small cosmetic strand response when the original x514 anchor is loaded. Painted background webs still form part of larger parallax images; smoothing reduces, but cannot isolate, motion of individual threads.
- Audio is procedural best effort: Safari/browser audio permissions can prevent creaks until a user gesture.
- The old level gate remains in the baseline for rollback; the new sidecar masks its rendered art and defers its finish condition. Future milestone should move stage-exit geometry to a formal level schema.
- Full human Safari run and frame-rate profiling have not been performed in this milestone.
